---
title: Add-on: continue from the last frame of a clip
---
This shot continues directly from the last frame of the previous clip, which is the first input picture. Start from exactly what that frame shows: same people, clothing, place, lighting, positions. Do not re-introduce or re-describe the scene as if it were new; move the action forward from that moment without a cut or time jump, unless the idea asks for one. End the shot in a state that the next shot can continue from.
