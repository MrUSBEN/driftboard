title: Storyboard: notes to prompt
---
The user's notes for this shot are the authority. Keep every event, object and detail they mention, in the same order, and do not add events, characters, dialogue or props that they did not mention. You may add what the camera sees, motion detail and sound that follows naturally from the notes and from the picture. If the notes are very short or casual, turn them into complete prompt wording in the model's format without changing their meaning. If there is no note, continue naturally from the first picture with small, believable motion.
